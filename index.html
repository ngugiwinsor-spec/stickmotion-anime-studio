Inverse kinematics · v5.2

Choose a three-part chain: upper limb (root), lower limb (middle), and hand/foot (end). The root must be an unparented layer for this solver. The solver adjusts the two joint angles to reach a stage position.
Target coordinates are relative to the stage centre. Use Auto-detect after naming and attaching your limb parts. The root must be unparented.

How to rig

1. Prepare separate transparent PNGs (head, torso, upper arm, forearm, thigh, shin, etc.).
2. Import the parts. Arrange them on the stage.
3. Set each pivot where the joint should rotate (e.g. elbow or shoulder).
4. Capture key poses on the timeline.
5. Export your rig JSON to save the setup.
